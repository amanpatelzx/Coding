SPL at REC Banda: The Missing Efficiency

Every year at REC Banda, the much awaited SPL (Sports Premier League) takes place. This year, n teams are participating in the grand football tournament. The rules state that each team must play exactly one match against every other team.

Aman and Akash are managing the tournament scoreboards. After every match, they record the goals scored by both teams. To determine the overall dominance of the teams, they calculate an efficiency score for each team. 
**The efficiency of a team is defined as the total number of goals they scored throughout the tournament minus the total number of goals they opponent scored.**

After the SPL ends, Aman successfully calculates the efficiency of all the teams. However, during the closing ceremony, Akash accidentally misplaced a part of the scorecard, and the efficiency of exactly one team went missing!

Given the efficiency of the remaining $n-1$ teams: $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots, a_{n-1}$, your task is to help them find the efficiency of the missing team. It is guaranteed that the efficiency of the missing team can be uniquely determined.


The first line contains an integer $t$ — the number of test cases.

The description of each test case follows:

   The first line contains a single integer $n$ — the number of teams participating in the SPL.

   The second line contains $n-1$ space-separated integers $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots, a_{n-1}$ — the efficiency of the $n-1$ teams whose scores were not lost.


   $1 \le t \le 500$

$2 \le n \le 100$

$-100 \le a_i \le 100$



For each test case, output a single line containing an integer representing the efficiency of the missing team.
